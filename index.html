Female Pubertal Development

Milestones & Tanner Staging

  • 6-8 Years: Adrenarche starts, body odor and pubic hair.
  • 8-9 Years: Thelarche starts (breast budding).
  • 10-11 Years: Growth spurt begins; Peak height velocity at 11-12 years.
  • 12-13 Years: Menarche (first menstruation); growth slows down.
  • 15-16 Years: Pubertal maturation & approaching adult height.
