Module 01

Growing Up: Physical & Emotional Changes

Adolescent Physical Changes & Tanner Staging

Puberty involves predictable developmental stages for both males and females, marked by height velocity, adrenarche, and sexual maturation[cite: 5, 6].

Female Development Timeline

Adrenarche (6-8 yrs) $\rightarrow$ Thelarche/Breast Budding (8-9 yrs) $\rightarrow$ Growth Spurt & Peak Height Velocity (10-12 yrs) $\rightarrow$ Menarche (12-13 yrs) $\rightarrow$ Pubertal Maturation (15-16 yrs)[cite: 5].

Male Development Timeline

Adrenarche (6-9 yrs) $\rightarrow$ Testicular Enlargement (10-11 yrs) $\rightarrow$ Pubic Hair & Penile Growth (11-12 yrs) $\rightarrow$ Peak Height Velocity & Spermarche/Voice Deepens (13-14 yrs) $\rightarrow$ Muscle Mass & Pubertal Maturation (15-18 yrs)[cite: 6].

Emotional & Psychosocial Changes

Adolescence shifts thinking patterns from childhood toward independent reasoning, identity development, and emotional regulation[cite: 7].

  • Emotional Shifts: Stronger, more variable emotions, heightened sensitivity to rejection, and desire for privacy[cite: 7].
  • Cognitive Growth: Abstract and hypothetical thinking, improved problem-solving, though executive functions continue maturing[cite: 7].
  • Identity Exploration: Navigating family boundaries, peer influence, self-esteem, body image, and romantic interest[cite: 8, 9].
Module 02

Taking Care of Yourself: Hygiene Practices

Body Odor & Sweat

  • • Shower daily with warm water and soap to manage active apocrine glands[cite: 10].
  • • Apply antiperspirant or deodorant daily for underarm care[cite: 10].

Skin Care & Acne

  • • Wash face twice daily with a gentle cleanser to combat increased oil production[cite: 10].
  • • Avoid popping pimples to prevent permanent scarring[cite: 10].

Genital & Menstrual Hygiene

  • • Clean vulva externally with plain warm water only; vagina self-cleans naturally without douching
